Estimates are provisional; accepted work evidences only the arguments actually assessed. New post-review additions require independent rechecking.

## MAT-230-u01: Normed and Banach spaces

- State the norm axioms and verify them for the sup norm on C_∞(X).
- Explain why convergence in the sup norm is uniform convergence and why C_∞(X) is complete.
- Show by explicit computation that the sup norm and the integral norm on C([0,1]) are not equivalent, and name the finite-dimensional hypothesis that fails.
- Produce a Cauchy sequence in (C([0,1]), ‖·‖₁) with no continuous limit.

Exit/synthesis: `MAT-230-u01-ex4`. Unit scheduled estimate: 315 minutes, including reading and workshops.

- `MAT-230-u01-ex1` — Two norms on C([0,1]); 35 minutes; independent.
- `MAT-230-u01-ex2` — Completeness fails for the integral norm; 40 minutes; counterexample.
- `MAT-230-u01-ex3` — What completeness buys; 15 minutes; independent.
- `MAT-230-u01-ex4` — Completeness depends on both the space and the norm; 70 minutes; synthesis.
## MAT-230-u02: Series, bounded operators and the dual

- Compute or bound operator norms, including for an integral operator, and prove the submultiplicative inequality.
- Explain why B(V,W) is a Banach space when W is, and why the dual space is always Banach.

## MAT-230-u03: Subspaces, quotients and Baire category

- Define the quotient of a normed space by a closed subspace and explain why the quotient norm is a norm.
- State the Baire category theorem precisely and use it to show a Banach space has no countable Hamel basis.
- State the uniform boundedness principle with its completeness hypothesis and give an example showing the hypothesis cannot be dropped.

Exit/synthesis: `MAT-230-u03-ex4`. Unit scheduled estimate: 315 minutes, including reading and workshops.

- `MAT-230-u03-ex1` — Closed subspaces and a dense one; 35 minutes; independent.

- State Zorn's lemma precisely and use it to show every vector space has a Hamel basis.
