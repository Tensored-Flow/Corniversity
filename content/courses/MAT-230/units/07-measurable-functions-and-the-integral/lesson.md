## Outline: Measurable functions, simple functions and the integral

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Lectures 9–11 of the MIT 18.102 Spring 2021 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keeping the notes' statement numbering so that every claim can be cited to an exact page.

**Scope.** Measurable functions and their closure properties; simple functions and approximation; the integral of nonnegative functions; monotone convergence and Fatou.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
