## Outline: Compact operators and the spectrum

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Lectures 19–20 of the MIT 18.102 Spring 2021 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keeping the notes' statement numbering so that every claim can be cited to an exact page.

**Scope.** Compact subsets of a Hilbert space; finite-rank operators and their norm limits; compact operators; the spectrum and resolvent of a bounded operator.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
