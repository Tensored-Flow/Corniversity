## Outline: Riesz representation and adjoints

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Lectures 17–18 of the MIT 18.102 Spring 2021 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keeping the notes' statement numbering so that every claim can be cited to an exact page.

**Scope.** Minimisers over closed convex sets; orthogonal complements and projections; the Riesz representation theorem; the adjoint of a bounded operator on a Hilbert space.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
