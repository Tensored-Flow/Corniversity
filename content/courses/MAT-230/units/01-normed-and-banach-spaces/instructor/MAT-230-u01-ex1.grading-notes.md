## Grading notes (instructor only)

- Automatic checks: `sup-norm-one` and `l1-norm` detect the two values; `no-equivalence-claim` flags the over-generalisation; `examples-only` flags lists of instances without a general argument. These are advisory; read the argument.
- Accept any correct family in place of $x^n$ if the learner argues generally; the prompt says "let $f_n=x^n$" but a correct alternative shows understanding and should be marked as an alternate approach, with a note that the set task used $x^n$.
- First-gap localisation: the most common first gap is the direction of the inequality in part 2. Quote the line and state which direction fails.
- Confidence: high when both values and the limit argument appear; medium when the conclusion is right but the limit argument is implicit; route to `needs_review` if the learner uses measure-theoretic language (a.e. equality) that the course has not yet introduced, since that may be correct but outside the unit's evidence.
- Do not penalise notation differences ($\|\cdot\|_{L^1}$, $\|\cdot\|_{\sup}$, $\int|f|$ without $dx$).
