## Outline: Jacobi fields, conjugate points and comparison

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Gudmundsson's chapter 9 (version 1.438, reference-only), keeping the notes' statement numbering (Definitions 9.1 and 9.3, Propositions 9.2 and 9.5, Examples 9.10–9.16, Lemma 9.17, Theorem 9.18), with Wendl §6.4 as the open-licensed alternative and Lee chapters 10–11 as the commercial reference.

**Scope.** One-parameter families of geodesics and their variation fields; the Jacobi equation $\nabla_X\nabla_XJ+R(J,X)X=0$ along a geodesic with tangent $X$, derived from the vanishing of $\nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma$ in the family; existence and uniqueness given initial value and derivative; explicit Jacobi fields in Euclidean space, on the sphere ($\sin$ and $\cos$ of arc length over $R$) and in the hyperbolic disc ($\sinh$, $\cosh$); conjugate points as zeros of Jacobi fields vanishing at the base point and their relation to the differential of the exponential map; the distance comparison theorem of the notes (Theorem 9.18): under a sectional-curvature bound the exponential map compares distances with the constant-curvature model space. The second variation formula and Riccati comparison, treated in Lee chapters 10–11, are stated with reference only until the book is acquired.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
