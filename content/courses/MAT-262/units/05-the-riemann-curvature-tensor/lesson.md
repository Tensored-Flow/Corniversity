## Outline: the Riemann curvature tensor

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Gudmundsson's chapter 8 (version 1.438, reference-only), keeping the notes' statement numbering (Definitions 8.1, 8.14, 8.27; Theorems 8.9, 8.29), with Sternberg §3.7 and §3.11–3.13 and Wendl §6.1 as open-licensed alternatives and Lee chapter 7 as the commercial reference.

**Scope.** Covariant derivatives of tensor fields; the curvature operator $R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z$, its tensoriality and the sign convention used in this course (stated explicitly, since conventions differ between sources); the symmetries of $R$ and the first Bianchi identity; sectional curvature $K(X,Y)=g(R(X,Y)Y,X)/(|X|^2|Y|^2-g(X,Y)^2)$ as a function on 2-planes and its coordinate expression; Ricci and scalar curvature by contraction; flat manifolds and the statement that $R\equiv0$ characterises local isometry to Euclidean space; constant sectional curvature and the formula $R(X,Y)Z=\kappa(g(Y,Z)X-g(X,Z)Y)$ (Theorem 8.29); the curvature of spheres ($\kappa=R^{-2}$ for the sphere of radius $R$, computed from the Christoffel symbols of unit 2), hyperbolic space and bi-invariant metrics on Lie groups.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
