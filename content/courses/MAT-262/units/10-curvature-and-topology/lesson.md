## Outline: curvature and topology

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Gudmundsson's Theorems 7.34, 8.29 and 9.18 and Appendix C (version 1.438, reference-only) and Sternberg's §9.3 (CC BY-NC-SA 2.0), with Lee chapter 12 as the commercial reference. The Cartan–Hadamard and Myers theorems are not proved in the open sources recorded for this course; the prepared lesson will prove them and label the proofs as Corniversity's own, citing Lee for the reader who has the book.

**Scope.** Manifolds of constant sectional curvature and the local classification by the model spaces; complete manifolds and the existence of geodesics between points (Theorem 7.34) as the input to global arguments; the distance comparison theorem (Theorem 9.18) as the model of a comparison argument; the Cartan–Hadamard theorem (a complete simply connected manifold of nonpositive sectional curvature is diffeomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^n$ via the exponential map) with its proof through the absence of conjugate points; Myers's theorem (a complete manifold with Ricci curvature bounded below by a positive constant is compact with finite fundamental group) with its proof through the second variation; Riemannian submersions and O'Neill's formulas as a source of positively curved examples. The unit feeds the synthesis project, which asks for one of these theorems in full with a note on what changes in Lorentzian signature.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
