## Outline: Lorentzian metrics in contrast

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Sternberg's chapter 7 (§7.1–7.2) and §8.1 (CC BY-NC-SA 2.0), with Lee's sections on pseudo-Riemannian metrics and model pseudo-Riemannian manifolds (chapters 2 and 3, per the author-hosted table of contents) as the commercial reference. The primary Lund notes do not treat indefinite metrics, and this unit says so.

**Scope.** Non-degenerate symmetric bilinear forms of signature $(-,+,+,+)$ and the convention of this course (the physics convention $(+,-,-,-)$ is named and not used); Minkowski space, Lorentz transformations, the causal character of vectors (timelike, null, spacelike), proper time along timelike curves and the reverse triangle inequality; the definition of a Lorentzian manifold. Then a systematic audit of the Riemannian course: the Koszul formula, Levi-Civita connection, Christoffel symbols, geodesics and the curvature tensor carry over verbatim because they use only non-degeneracy; arc length, the distance function, Hopf–Rinow and the partition-of-unity existence theorem do not, and the obstruction to the existence of Lorentzian metrics on compact manifolds is stated. The unit closes by restating why the course keeps the two cases apart.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
