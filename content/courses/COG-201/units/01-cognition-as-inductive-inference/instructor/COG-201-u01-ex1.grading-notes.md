## Grading notes (instructor only)

- Automatic checks detect the $3^k$ count and the two sample sizes; they are advisory. A learner using the exact $-\ln(1-\varepsilon)$ denominator will fail both numeric checks while being correct: read the working before trusting a failed check.
- First-gap localisation: the commonest first gap is the step from "one bad hypothesis" to "some bad hypothesis" (the union bound). Quote the line where the learner concludes about *the* consistent hypothesis and ask how many hypotheses could have been consistent.
- The assumptions must be named in part 1, not only in part 4; the exit check asks for it. If they appear only in part 4, mark `bad-hypothesis-bound` partially met.
- Accept a count of conjunctions that excludes negated literals ($2^k$) only if the learner says so and adjusts the numbers consistently ($10(10\ln2+\ln20)=99.3\to100$).
- Confidence: high when the proof, counts and numbers are all present; medium when the union bound is implicit; route to `needs_review` if the learner brings in VC dimension or an infinite hypothesis space, which is beyond the unit's evidence though possibly correct.
