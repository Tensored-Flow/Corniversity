## Grading notes (instructor only)

- Automatic checks detect $P(\text{trick}\mid D)\approx0.082$, the posterior $\mathrm{Beta}(5,9)$ and the predictive $5/14$. They are advisory; a learner who writes the posterior as "Beta with parameters 5 and 9" will fail the pattern while being correct. Read the working.
- First-gap localisation: in part 1 the usual gap is multiplying the likelihood ratio by $1/1000$ instead of by the prior *odds* $1/999$ (a negligible numerical difference here, but a conceptual one; comment precisely without penalising the arithmetic). In part 3 the usual gap is the posterior parameters; quote the line and ask what the prior's fictitious counts were.
- Part 4 is the discriminating part. Accept any sound argument for $P(D\mid\text{fair})\approx2^{-25}$ and any correct statement that the exact value is larger; the Jensen/convexity reason is ideal but "each factor exceeds one half" suffices for `met`.
- Do not penalise rounding differences at the third significant figure caused by using $\ln1.9=0.642$ versus $0.6419$.
- Confidence: high when all four parts have working; medium when part 4 is approximate without direction; route to `needs_review` if the learner proposes a different hierarchical structure (for example a mixture prior on $\theta$ written as a single density) and reaches a different number, since the modelling may be defensible.
