## Outline: catalysis and applying chemical principles

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from lectures 34–35 of MIT 5.111SC (Fall 2014, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), with OpenStax Chemistry 2e section 12.7 as the linked reading, citing lecture-notes pages exactly as the prepared units do.

**Scope.** What a catalyst does to a mechanism (a new path with a lower activation energy for both directions) and what it cannot do (change $\Delta G^\circ$, $K$ or the equilibrium composition), continuing unit 5's reaction-coordinate picture; homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysis with the examples OpenStax 12.7 treats; enzymes as catalysts and the engineering of enzyme pathways that lecture 34's videos describe, treated as case studies rather than as procedures; lecture 35's closing synthesis of the course's principles.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics (a reaction-coordinate analysis of a catalysed versus uncatalysed path with the Arrhenius consequences computed, and a written explanation separating what catalysis changes from what it does not), instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
