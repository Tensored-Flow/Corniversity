# Temperature interpolation and a failed extrapolation — reference reasoning

Ea=R ln4/(1/290−1/315)=42.117 kJ mol⁻¹ (R8.31446). ln(k302/.015)=Ea/R(1/290−1/302)=0.69406, so k302≈0.0300276 s⁻¹. The same fit predicts k340≈0.19573 s⁻¹, whereas measured .0500 is below k315; a constant positive-Ea model cannot reproduce all exact values. Possible causes: deactivation, mechanism/speciation changes, incorrect temperature calibration, or measurement uncertainty. For deactivation compare freshly prepared material and temperature-preincubated material at a common assay temperature with controls; recovery and time dependence help discriminate. Numerical disagreement alone does not identify which cause occurred.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
