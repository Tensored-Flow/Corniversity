# Can the curve identify the mechanism? — reference reasoning

KM=(k−1+12)/k1. Choose k1=1.0e6 M⁻¹s⁻¹ giving k−1=18 s⁻¹; choose 2.0e6 giving 48 s⁻¹. Both yield 30 μM though KD differs 18 versus 24 μM. α=1+40/20=3, apparent KM90 μM. Ratio=(KM+S)/(αKM+S); at 30,60/120=.5; at 300,330/390=11/13≈.84615. Unchanged limiting Vmax and these ratios depend on the free-enzyme competitive/rapid-inhibitor-equilibrium model with fixed free inhibitor; the rate curve alone cannot prove it. A direct binding/transient measurement can constrain k−1 or KD, or several inhibitor/substrate conditions can test the competitive curve. Distinguish measured total inhibitor from assumed free concentration.
