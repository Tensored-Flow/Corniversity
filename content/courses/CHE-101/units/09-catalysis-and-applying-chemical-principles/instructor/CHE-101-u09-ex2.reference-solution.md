# Derive, then calculate, an enzyme curve — reference reasoning

0≈k1(ET−ES)S−(k−1+k2)ES gives ES=ET S/(S+KM), KM=(60+20)/(4e6)=20 μM. KD=60/(4e6)=15 μM. Vmax=k2ET=4.0 μM s⁻¹. v5=.8, v20=2.0, v100=10/3≈3.333 μM s⁻¹. k2 is one third of k−1, so replacing KM by KD would be an extra non-negligible approximation (25% below KM). Need quasi-steady ES after transient, near-constant substrate and enzyme total, negligible product reverse step, initial conditions and appropriate homogeneous concentrations. Here ET/(KM+S)≤.008 supports but does not by itself establish the regime.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
