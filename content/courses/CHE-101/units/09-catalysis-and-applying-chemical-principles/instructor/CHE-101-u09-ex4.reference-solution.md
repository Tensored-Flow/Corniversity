# A catalyst claim under cross-examination — reference reasoning

dB/dt=kfC−(kf+kr)B. Beq=.0750 M, Aeq=.0250, K=3. B(t)=.075[1−exp(−.0400t)] without catalyst and .075[1−exp(−.200t)] with it. t90=ln10/.04=57.5646 min versus 11.5129 min. At 20 min B=.0413003 versus .0736263 M. The equilibrium endpoint is unchanged though finite-time composition differs. Run matched-temperature, same-total-concentration trials from A-rich and B-rich starts with/without catalyst, monitor both species and conservation, use blanks/calibrated measurements and replicate time courses until agreement and stable endpoints. Faster common-endpoint convergence supports a rate effect; distinct reproducible reversible endpoints require changed chemistry/conditions or other explanation. Pre-age catalyst and test activity, and sample late to detect deactivation. Side reactions, adsorption sequestering a species, temperature changes, nonideal activities and transport violate the simple two-state interpretation. Mere apparent plateaux can be kinetic arrest.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
