## Grading notes (instructor only)

- Automatic checks: `balanced-equation` requires all three coefficients (3 O2, 2 CO2, 3 H2O) in the normalised text; `moles-co2`, `mass-co2` and `mass-balance-total` look for the values 0.499, 21.97 and 35.46 within tolerance. They are advisory; a correct answer that rounds differently (for example to two significant figures, 22 g and 35 g) can fail the last check and still be accepted on the rubric.
- This is a diagnostic. If the attempt is clearly fluent (balanced, units carried, mass balance shown), accept and record the evidence promptly; do not extend the tutorial. If it fails, name the single step that failed and point to the matching OpenStax section (4.1 for balancing, 3.1 for moles, 4.3 for the ratio), then invite a second attempt.
- First-gap localisation: the most common first gap is comparing or converting grams without passing through moles. Quote the line.
- Do not penalise notation differences (→ versus =, with or without phase labels in the working, as long as phases appear once).
- Confidence: high when the three numeric values and the balance appear; medium when the method is right with an arithmetic slip; route to `needs_review` if the learner balances a different equation (for example to CO) and argues it is also valid, since that is a chemistry question about products, not a bookkeeping error.
