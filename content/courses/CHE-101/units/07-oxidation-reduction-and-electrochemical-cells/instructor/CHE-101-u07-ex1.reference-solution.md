# Balance without losing the medium — reference reasoning

Mn reduction in acid: MnO₄⁻ + 4H⁺ + 3e⁻ → MnO₂ + 2H₂O; in base: MnO₄⁻ + 2H₂O + 3e⁻ → MnO₂ + 4OH⁻. Sulfite oxidation in base is SO₃²⁻ + 2OH⁻ → SO₄²⁻ + H₂O + 2e⁻. Multiply the first by 2 and second by 3 and cancel: 2MnO₄⁻ + 3SO₃²⁻ + H₂O → 2MnO₂ + 3SO₄²⁻ + 2OH⁻. Left and right contain Mn2 S3 O18 H2 and charge −8. Mn goes +7→+4, accepting 6 electrons overall; S +4→+6 supplies 6. Permanganate is oxidant; sulfite reductant. Product speciation depends on pH, potentials, concentrations and competing reactions; the prompt stipulates MnO₂.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
