# Potential is not multiplied — reference reasoning

n=2, E°=1.103 V and Q=20. E=1.103−(RT/2F)ln20=1.06452 V. ΔrG=−2F E≈−205.42 kJ mol⁻¹ of the reaction as written. log₁₀K=2F(1.103)/(2.302585RT)≈37.2890. The cell is favourable forward at this composition. Doubling gives n'=4, Q'=400, K'=K², ΔrG'=2ΔrG; E° and E stay fixed because lnQ'/n'=lnQ/n. Electrode potentials themselves are not multiplied.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
