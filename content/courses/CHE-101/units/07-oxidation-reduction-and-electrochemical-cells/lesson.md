## Outline: oxidation–reduction and electrochemical cells

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from lectures 25–26 of MIT 5.111SC (Fall 2014, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), with OpenStax Chemistry 2e sections 4.2 and 17.1–17.4 as the linked reading, citing lecture-notes pages exactly as the prepared units do.

**Scope.** Oxidation numbers and the half-reaction method for balancing redox equations in acidic and basic solution (mass and charge balance, as always, first); galvanic cells, electrodes and cell notation; standard reduction potentials and the standard hydrogen electrode; $E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cathode}} - E^\circ_{\text{anode}}$ and the spontaneity criterion; $\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ$ and the link to $K$ from unit 4; the Nernst equation for non-standard conditions; the biological oxidations that lecture 26 uses as examples, treated as models with stated assumptions.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics (one half-reaction balancing problem with a charge-balance check, one cell-potential and $K$ calculation with standard states and temperature stated), instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
