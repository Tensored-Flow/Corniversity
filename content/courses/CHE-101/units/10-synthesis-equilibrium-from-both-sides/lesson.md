## Outline: equilibrium from both sides

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It is the synthesis-week unit and will be written from pages already verified in the prepared units: lecture 18 pages 1–2 (ΔG = ΔG° + RT ln Q, ΔG° = −RT ln K), lecture 31 page 4 (K = k_f/k_r for elementary steps) and lecture 33 page 6 (how rate constants and equilibrium constants respond to temperature) of MIT 5.111SC (Fall 2014, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), with OpenStax Chemistry 2e 13.1 and 16.4 as linked reading.

**Scope.** Two derivations of the same equilibrium constant, one from free energy and one from equal rates, and the conditions under which they agree (elementary steps, ideal behaviour, the same reference states); why only temperature changes $K$ in both pictures; what the van 't Hoff and Arrhenius equations share and how they differ; one system chosen by the learner (the reversible model of unit 5 is one option) worked through from both sides with every assumption written down.

**What preparation will add.** Guidance for the written synthesis (assessment a4), a checklist of assumptions, one worked bridge between the two views, and the common misconceptions that the earlier units recorded. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
