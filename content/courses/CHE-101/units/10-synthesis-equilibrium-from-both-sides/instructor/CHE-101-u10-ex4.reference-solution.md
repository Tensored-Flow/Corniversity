# Cumulative capstone: audit a carbon-conversion proposal — reference reasoning

A complete report must connect all parts; accept equivalent organisation and physically equivalent conventions.

(a) C1 O2 H2 on both sides; +2 from protons cancels −2 from electrons. Carbon changes+4 in CO₂ to+2 in HCOOH (two H+1 and two O−2), hence two electrons per carbon. CO₂ has two C=O regions, no central lone pair in Lewis/VSEPR, linear 180°, bond dipoles cancel; the molecule is nonpolar in that symmetric model.

(b) Q=.0100/(1×.0100²)=100. Ered=−.200−(RT/2F)ln100≈−.259159V. Ecell=Ecathode−Eanode=−.359159V; forward cell ΔrG=−2FEcell≈+69.3069kJmol⁻¹. The paired donor half-reaction was not specified chemically, so this is per mole of the stipulated two-electron cell process; it cannot supply a complete atom-balanced overall cell reaction or identify donor products. Minimum reversible electrical input at that instantaneous state is 69.31kJ per mole converted, excluding real losses. The standard E° value is pedagogical supplied data, not asserted real CO₂ electrode data. A changing composition would require integrating the reversible potential along charge transfer.

(c) Aminus/HA=10^(2−3.75)=.0177828; neutral fraction 1/(1+.0177828)=.9825279 and anion fraction.0174721. With neutral activity.0100, ideal total acid concentration≈.0101778M. Faradaic production means total acid equivalents, whose partition follows pH; reservoir control or sufficiently small conversion would be needed to maintain all fixed activities. A closed unbuffered run cannot simply assume constant pH, neutral product activity and CO₂ activity.

(d) q=.5×3600=1800C, electron moles=.01865575, total acid-equivalent moles=.8q/(2F)=.00746230mol; equivalent mass=.343452g. Supply energy 1.5×1800=2700J. Energy per mole intended product=2700/.00746230=361818.75Jmol⁻¹≈361.819kJmol⁻¹. This differs from reversible demand because of voltage above reversible level, charge diverted to other reactions, and changing states/other losses. Charge-based amount is conditional on the stipulated efficiency, not independent verification of it.

(e) d count 10−2=8, CN4, complex formal charge+2 if all donors neutral. Strongly split square-planar d8 may be diamagnetic; ordinary high-spin tetrahedral e4t2^4 has two unpaired. Evidence favours the former under these models, but magnetism alone does not uniquely establish structure; seek diffraction or complementary structural spectroscopy and check purity/temperature effects.

(f) Catalyst changes pathway/rates, not K of the same overall reaction at fixed conditions. Temperature dependence of K follows reaction enthalpy; an exothermic forward reaction can have decreasing K even as its positive-Ea rate rises. Current includes side reactions/capacitive contributions and does not prove carbon product identity. Use calibrated product analysis with blanks, independent charge integration, measured reactant consumption and carbon balance, replicate controlled pH/T trials, catalyst-free and aged-catalyst controls, and time courses from both sides where a reversible chemical equilibrium experiment is physically meaningful. An electrically driven maintained cell is not itself the same as a closed equilibrium experiment; state that limitation. Recompute Faradaic efficiency from independently measured total acid equivalents, monitor side products and catalyst retention/turnover. Limits may include activity coefficients, gas transport, overpotential, donor chemistry, deactivation and measurement uncertainty. The report should not claim real-device performance from the model.

Blueprint: criterion 1 tests early atom/bonding and conservation; criterion 2 thermodynamics/electrochemistry; criterion 3 acid speciation; criterion 4 stoichiometric/energy numeracy; criterion 5 coordination-model inference; criterion 6 kinetic/equilibrium and experimental reasoning. Address all six; a sound calculation in one cannot substitute for missing integration elsewhere.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
