# Three counts, two geometries — reference reasoning

[Co(en)₃]³⁺: metal+3, d⁶, six donor atoms and CN6 despite three ligand molecules. [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻: x−6=−4 gives Fe+2, d⁶, CN6. [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂] neutral: x−2=0 gives Pt+2, d⁸, CN4. In square-planar cis form the two chlorides occupy adjacent vertices, e.g +x,+y; trans has opposite +x,−x. The NH₃ occupy remaining vertices. A planar 90° rotation cannot turn adjacent vertices into opposite ones. CN counts metal–donor attachments, not merely molecular ligands.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
