# Choose a structure, then limit the claim — reference reasoning

M is+2, d⁸, CN4. In a standard high-spin tetrahedral model e⁴t₂⁴ has two unpaired electrons; equivalent tetrahedral vertices give only one arrangement for MA₂B₂ of simple monodentate ligands. A strongly split square-planar d⁸ model pairs eight electrons in four lower orbitals and supports cis/trans MA₂B₂, fitting both observations. For tetrahedral lower a and upper b:2a+3b=0 and b−a=Δt imply a=−3Δt/5,b=2Δt/5. Limits include unusual orbital ordering/covalency, magnetic interactions or temperature effects, mixtures, and additional ligand stereochemistry beyond the simple model. Single-crystal diffraction (or well-specified complementary structural spectroscopy) can test donor geometry. Formation K describes ΔG of equilibrium, while ligand-exchange speed depends on kinetic barriers; thermodynamic stability does not imply fast or slow exchange without more evidence.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
