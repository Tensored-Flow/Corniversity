## Outline: acid–base equilibria

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from lectures 20–24 of MIT 5.111SC (Fall 2014, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), with OpenStax Chemistry 2e sections 14.1–14.3, 14.6 and 14.7 as the linked reading, citing lecture-notes pages exactly as the prepared units do.

**Scope.** The Brønsted–Lowry definitions and conjugate pairs; the autoionisation of water, $K_w$ and the pH scale; $K_a$, $K_b$ and $K_aK_b = K_w$; equilibrium calculations for weak acids and bases with and without simplifying assumptions, stated as such; hydrolysis of salts; buffers and the Henderson–Hasselbalch relation with its range of validity; titration curves for strong and weak systems and the choice of indicator. The equilibrium machinery of unit 4 is applied throughout; every constant will carry its temperature (25 °C unless stated) and reference state.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics (including one weak-acid ICE calculation with the approximation checked numerically and one buffer problem), instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
