# An acid solution with pH near seven — reference reasoning

The spectator anion concentration is $C$, so charge balance is $h=C+[OH^-]$. With $[OH^-]=K_w/h$, $h^2-Ch-K_w=0$. The positive root gives $h=1.05124922\times10^{-7}$ M, pH $=6.97829$. It exceeds the neutral hydronium concentration; pH 8 would require fewer positive ions than the water/anion charge balance permits. For $C\ll\sqrt{K_w}$, expansion gives $h=\sqrt{K_w}+C/2+O(C^2/\sqrt{K_w})$. For $C\gg\sqrt{K_w}$, $h=C+K_w/C+O(K_w^2/C^3)$, recovering the strong-acid shortcut. Full dissociation does not imply water can be ignored.
