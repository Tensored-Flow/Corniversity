# When the square-root shortcut fails — reference reasoning

Mass balance is $[HA]+[A^-]=C$; neglecting water, $[A^-]=h$. Thus $h^2+K_ah-K_aC=0$. The shortcut gives $h_s=0.001000$ M, while $h=(-0.000100+\sqrt{0.00000401})/2=0.000951249$ M. Therefore pH $=3.0217$ and ionisation $=9.51249\%$. The shortcut overestimates $h$ by $5.125\%$ relative to the root and understates pH by $0.0217$. The roughly ten-percent consumption of HA makes the small-dissociation assumption poor at a five-percent tolerance. Water contributes $K_w/h=1.05\times10^{-11}$ M hydroxide, negligible against $h$; the quadratic therefore remains a consistent model.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
