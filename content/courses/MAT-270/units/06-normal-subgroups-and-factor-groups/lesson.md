## Outline: Normal subgroups and factor groups

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from chapter 10 of Judson's Abstract Algebra: Theory and Applications (HTML edition, GFDL), citing each statement by its number so that every claim can be reopened at its section page.

**Scope.** Normal subgroups as those with $gN=Ng$ for all $g$, and the equivalent conditions $gNg^{-1}\subset N$ and $gNg^{-1}=N$ (Theorem 10.1.3); why the coset multiplication $(aN)(bN)=abN$ is well defined exactly when $N$ is normal, and the factor group $G/N$ of order $[G:N]$ (Theorem 10.1.4); examples ($\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\cong\mathbb Z_n$, $A_n\lhd S_n$, the centre, every subgroup of an abelian group, every subgroup of index two) and non-examples (a subgroup of $S_3$ of order two); the simplicity of $A_n$ for $n\ge5$ (Section 10.2), with $3$-cycles generating $A_n$ as the key lemma.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with the well-definedness argument written out and the index-two case proved, multiplication tables of small factor groups, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
