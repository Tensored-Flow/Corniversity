## Grading notes (instructor only)

This is an explain-back; grade the ideas, not the prose.

- Part 1 must contain: a subgroup is a subset that is a group *under the operation of the ambient group*; $GL_2(\mathbb R)$ is a group under multiplication (closure via $\det(AB)=\det A\det B\neq0$, inverses by invertibility); under addition it fails closure, witnessed by $A+(-A)=0\notin GL_2(\mathbb R)$ (or $I+I=2I$ is fine, but $I+(-I)$ is the cleanest since $0$ is never invertible); so it is not a subgroup of $(M_2(\mathbb R),+)$. A learner who says only "the operations differ" without exhibiting the failure of closure is partially met.
- Part 2 must contain: in $(\mathbb Z_n,\cdot)$ the element $0$ has no inverse (and any $a$ with $\gcd(a,n)>1$ has none, by Proposition 3.1.4); $U(n)$ keeps only the units, inherits associativity and the identity $1$, and the condition that must be proved is closure (product of units is a unit, via $(ab)(b^{-1}a^{-1})=1$). Mentioning that $0\notin U(n)$ because $\gcd(0,n)=n>1$ is a nice touch, not required.
- Flag as errors: "$\mathbb Z_n$ is a group under multiplication for prime $n$" (false: $0$ still has no inverse; $\mathbb Z_p^*=U(p)$ is the group), or "a subset that is a group is automatically a subgroup".
- Keep feedback under 120 words.
