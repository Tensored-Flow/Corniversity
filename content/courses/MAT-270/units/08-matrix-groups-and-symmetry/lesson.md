## Outline: Matrix groups and symmetry

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from chapter 12 of Judson's Abstract Algebra: Theory and Applications (HTML edition, GFDL), citing each statement by its number so that every claim can be reopened at its section page.

**Scope.** $GL_n(\mathbb R)$ and $SL_n(\mathbb R)$ and the determinant homomorphism (unit 7 revisited); the Euclidean inner product (Proposition 12.1.6) and the orthogonal group $O(n)$, with the equivalence of orthonormal columns, $A^{-1}=A^{\mathsf T}$, preservation of inner products, of distances and of lengths (Theorem 12.1.8); $SO(n)$ and rotations versus reflections in the plane; isometries of $\mathbb R^2$ as compositions of orthogonal maps and translations; finite subgroups of $O(2)$ and the dihedral groups of unit 3; the wallpaper groups (Section 12.2), presented as classification without full proof.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with explicit matrices for rotations and reflections and the computation of their products, examples and non-examples (a length-preserving map that is not linear), original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
