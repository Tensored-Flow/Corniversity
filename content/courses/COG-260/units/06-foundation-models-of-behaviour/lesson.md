## Outline: Foundation models of human behaviour: Centaur and Psych-101

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**.

**Scope.** Psych-101 and what it contains; the information contract of the main test (familiar experiments, unseen participants within them); the generalisation tests (a modified cover story, a modified problem structure, a new domain, and six further paradigms); the published critiques; and which application claims the evidence warrants.

**What preparation will add.** A lesson, a seminar report under the programme's paper-seminar headings, and an exit check.
