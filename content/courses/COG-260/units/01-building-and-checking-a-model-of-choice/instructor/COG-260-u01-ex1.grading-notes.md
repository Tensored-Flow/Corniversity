## Grading notes (instructor only)

- **Part 1.** Accept a derivation that maximises $c\ln(1-\varepsilon/2)+(n-c)\ln(\varepsilon/2)$ directly, or one that first estimates the rule-following probability $p=1-\varepsilon/2$ as $c/n$ and then transforms it (invariance of the maximum-likelihood estimate). Full credit needs the trial-1 term named as constant and the boundary case $c<n/2$ mentioned. An estimate of $0.15$ comes from writing the noise as $\varepsilon$ rather than $\varepsilon/2$. That is a different model, so mark the criterion not met and point to the model's definition.
- **Part 2.** The common error is updating both arms, which leaves $Q_0=Q_1$ and gives $P=0.5$ in part 3. Point to the paper's Equation 3 and to the code difference discussed in the lesson.
- **Part 3.** $0.5117$ comes from dividing by $\beta$, which treats it as a temperature. Box 4's wording invites this. Credit the method but not the value, and ask the learner to reconcile it with Box 1's definition.
- **Part 4.** $241$ and $248$ (no penalty) or $\log_{10}$ penalties ($245.6$ and $250.3$) are errors. Note that the conclusion happens to survive both.
- **Part 5.** This is the part to press on. "Rescorla-Wagner is the true model" or "the participant learns from rewards" earns no credit without the in-sample caveat and a named next check: model recovery, validation by simulation, or held-out evaluation.
