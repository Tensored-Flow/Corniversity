## Grading notes (instructor only)

The report is the unit's exit check. Read it for discipline before results.

- **Order.** Predictions must be quoted from the notebook, with their time, before the corresponding results. A report that gives results first and "predictions" afterwards has not followed the loop. Ask for the saved note.
- **Contracts.** Every held-out number needs its ten points first, in the learner's words. Copying the laboratory's contract text is acceptable if every point is present and the split it belongs to is named. Missing points cap the criterion at partially met. The two most often missing are point 8 (unavailable in real use) and point 10 (claims not tested).
- **The random split.** It may be reported, but only as the illustration of a leak. Using it as evidence of prediction fails that criterion.
- **The ambiguous case.** Full credit needs "inconclusive" stated as the finding, an explanation in terms of near-identical predictions (low $\beta$, noisy win-stay lose-shift, a short session, close arms), a proposed design change, and a prediction of its effect on the confusion matrix made before any test. The laboratory's own runs show what matters: the participants' region drives the ambiguity, more trials help slowly, and a larger difference between the arms does not help (see the exercise 2 reference for the counts). A learner who predicts that wider arms will restore the diagonal, tests it and reports that it did not has done exactly what the section asks; credit the test and the honest report, not the prediction. Changing the region changes the participants rather than the design, and a learner who points this out deserves credit.
- **Limits.** Any sentence that attributes a mechanism to real people from this synthetic loop fails the limits criterion. The Eckstein et al. (2022) sentence should say that absolute values differed between tasks, that only noise or exploration and the learning rate for positive outcomes partly generalised, and only between similar tasks, and that the learning rate for negative outcomes did not.
- **Revision.** The revision should answer each feedback point. Look for changed text, not a covering note.
