## Outline: What a fitted parameter measures: reliability and generalisation

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**.

**Scope.** The difference between an effect that is reliable across people on average and a score that reliably ranks people; the intraclass correlation and its variance components; why subtracting conditions does not rescue a between-person signal; how far fitted learning parameters carry from one task to another for the same participants, and what that means for treating them as traits.

**What preparation will add.** A lesson, exercises on intraclass correlations and on reading generalisation results, and an exit check. Until then the reading listed for this unit is accurate and the tutor can discuss it, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
