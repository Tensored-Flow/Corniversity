## Grading notes (instructor only)

- Automatic checks detect the updated values $v=2.05$ and $w_2=0.95$; both wrong usually means a sign error in the update rule, one wrong usually means a dropped $x_2=-1$. They are advisory; read the sweep.
- Consistency rule: if a single early slip (for example $\sigma'$ taken as $\sigma$) is propagated correctly through the rest of the sweep, mark `adjoints` as partially met and localise the first gap at that line; do not deduct again downstream.
- First-gap localisation: the commonest first gap is $\bar z$. Quote the learner's line and ask what $\sigma'(0)$ is.
- The finite-difference check is required by the prompt; a learner who skips it has not met the exit check even if every adjoint is right, because the habit is the point. A one-sided difference is acceptable if the learner comments on its $O(\varepsilon)$ error.
- Accept matrix/Jacobian notation, autograd-style code that prints the gradients, or a fully symbolic derivation, provided the numbers are stated.
- Confidence: high when all eight adjoints, the update and the check are present; medium when the check is missing; route to `needs_review` if the learner reports a loss increase after the step together with correct-looking adjoints (something unusual happened; inspect).
