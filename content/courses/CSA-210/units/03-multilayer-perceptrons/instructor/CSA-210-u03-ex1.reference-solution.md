# A ReLU spline and its error — reference reasoning

Slopes are 1/2 then 3/2, so p (x)=x/2+ReLU(x−1/2). At 1/4 p=.125 versus f=.0625; at 3/4 p=.625 versus f=.5625; both over by 1/16. On [0,.5], p−x²=x(.5−x), maximum 1/16 at .25; on [.5,1], p−x²=(x−.5)(1−x), maximum 1/16 at .75. At −1, p=−.5 versus f1; at 2, p=2.5 versus f4. The approximation construction was restricted to a compact interval, so outside slopes continue affinely while x² curves. The linear term can be represented by .5ReLU(x)−.5ReLU(−x).
