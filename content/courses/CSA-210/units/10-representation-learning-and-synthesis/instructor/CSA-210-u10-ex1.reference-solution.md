# A good reconstruction can discard the label — reference reasoning

For the rank-one projector P = aaᵀ, expected squared reconstruction error is tr (C) − aᵀCa. With C = diag(9, 1) this equals 10 − 9cos²θ − sin²θ = 1 + 8sin²θ. Its minimum is 1 at either first-axis direction, a = ±(1, 0).

The retained coordinate ±U is independent of V and hence of Y. Symmetry and continuity give P(Y = 1) = 1/2, so no classifier using U alone can exceed population accuracy 1/2. Projection onto the second axis has reconstruction error 9 but retains V, which determines Y except at a zero-probability tie. The reconstruction and prediction objectives disagree.

The PCA connection assumes centred data, linear encoder/decoder maps, squared reconstruction loss, a suitable bottleneck and attainment of a global optimum. Latent coordinates can rotate or rescale while preserving the optimal reconstruction subspace. A finite empirical covariance also differs from the stipulated population covariance.
