## Outline: Representation learning without labels; synthesis project

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from W2D3 Tutorial 1 (Introduction to generative models) and W3D3 Tutorial 1 (Un/Self-supervised learning methods) of the Neuromatch Deep Learning ebook (content CC BY 4.0), cited by tutorial page; both pages were confirmed to load on 2026-10-30 but their bodies were truncated in the fetch, so section-level locators will be added when the unit is prepared.

**Scope.** Learning without labels: generative models as density estimation and sampling (what a likelihood, a reconstruction loss or an adversarial objective each optimise); self-supervised objectives that manufacture supervision from the data; and the evidence question that unit 1 began with, now for representations: a linear probe, a transfer task or a nearest-neighbour test each measures something different, and a representation's "quality" is relative to the downstream use and the baseline. The second half of the unit is the course synthesis: the final project brief (assessment a3), a reproducible notebook that trains a model on a small dataset, reports a baseline, states its split and seed policy, examines failure cases and explains the model's behaviour, with an out-of-sample or counterexample check.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson, a project brief with its rubric unpacked, original exercises (an evaluation-protocol design for a learned representation; a short derivation connecting a reconstruction objective to a likelihood under a Gaussian noise model), instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions ("a lower reconstruction loss means a better representation"). Until then the reading above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
