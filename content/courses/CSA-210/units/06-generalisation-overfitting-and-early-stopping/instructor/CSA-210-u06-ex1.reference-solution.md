# Bias–variance with the right source of randomness — reference reasoning

Write fD−Y=(fD−E fD)+(E fD−f*)−ε. Squaring and taking expectations makes cross terms 0 under centring and independence. Procedure 1 risk=.5²+.20+.50=.95. Procedure 2 risk=.1²+.60+.50=1.11. Smaller bias alone does not ensure lower total risk. Seeds on fixed D measure conditional algorithmic variability; the given variance is across independently sampled training datasets (and any procedure randomness included in its definition). The law of total variance separates these components, so they cannot simply be substituted.
