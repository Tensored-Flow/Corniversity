## Grading notes (instructor only)

This is an explain-back; grade the ideas, not the prose, and keep feedback under 120 words.

- Part 1 must contain the independence condition (held-out data independent of training data and of every decision used to fit the model) and the one-line reason: conditional on the fitted model the held-out losses are i.i.d. with mean equal to the risk, whereas the training error is the minimum the fitting procedure could reach on that sample, hence optimistic. Equating "unbiased" with "large sample" is a conceptual error worth a precise comment.
- Part 2 must say that the best-of-$m$ test error is the minimum of noisy estimates and so biased downward, and that the validation set exists precisely to absorb selection so the test set can be used once. "The validation set is for checking overfitting" alone is partially met.
- Part 3: any of temporal structure, grouped records, duplicates or augmented copies across splits, target-derived features, or preprocessing fitted on all data counts, provided the learner states the route by which information crosses the split. A random split is a correct answer to a different question (experimenter selection), which is the point.
- If the learner cites the unit 1 lab (feature selection outside the folds) as the example, accept it: it is leakage that survives a random split.
