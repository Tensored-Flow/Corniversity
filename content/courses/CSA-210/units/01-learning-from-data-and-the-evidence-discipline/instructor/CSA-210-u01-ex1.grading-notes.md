## Grading notes (instructor only)

- Automatic checks are advisory. `names-ticket-leak` only confirms the feature is mentioned, not that it is classified; `baseline-94` detects the majority baseline; `no-leak-denial` flags a submission that calls the pipeline sound. Read the argument.
- The exit check for the unit is this exercise. Accept when the target leak, at least two of the split and selection leaks, the 94% baseline and a protocol without test-set tuning are all present. The preprocessing leak may be omitted or called minor without penalty if the others are handled.
- First-gap localisation: the commonest first gap is treating the random shuffle as harmless. Quote the learner's line about step 3 and ask what the record for week $t+1$ of the same machine contains.
- Do not penalise a ranking that differs from the reference if the justification is coherent; do penalise a ranking that places standardisation above the ticket feature.
- A learner who writes "6% baseline" has inverted the base rate; this is a conceptual slip worth a precise comment, not a failing mark on its own.
- Confidence: high when the inventory, baseline and protocol are all explicit; medium when the protocol is present but vague on grouping; route to `needs_review` if the learner proposes a statistical correction for the selection bias (e.g. a Bonferroni-style adjustment) instead of a validation set, since that may be defensible but is outside the unit's evidence.
- Keep feedback to the specific lines; this is an audit, not an essay.
