# Grading notes

Require a precise cutoff, the retrospective-ticket violation, group/time and selection analysis, the 94% baseline and a corrected protocol. Literal duplicates, direct label access, ticket accuracy and the amount/order of inflation are not established. Accept conditional rankings and any defensible estimand-specific split. Do not impose the former unsupported rule that preprocessing must rank below the ticket. Check label-window availability as well as feature time. Automated pattern checks only detect mentions; they do not establish the reasoning.
