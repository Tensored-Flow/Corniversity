## Grading notes (instructor only)

- **Part 1.** Accept any correct route description. The common error in (c) is to count the right-hand corridor as passable. Point out that (1,8) and the door are both walls in the shortcut layout before the change.
- **Part 2.** $24$ means the bump was not counted as a move. In this laboratory a bump costs 1 and takes a time step. Credit the trace, but ask the learner to reconcile the count with the cost definition. $16$ means the learner assumed the planner already knew about the block, which is the point the condition tests.
- **Part 3.** The essential step is that while up stays greedy, the target is $-1+Q(\text{up})$, so the update is a fixed decrement of $\alpha$. Learners who iterate numerically and reach 5 and 21 earn full credit for the numbers. Learners who answer 4 and 20 have broken the tie the wrong way; point to the tie order in the lesson. The general condition $k>2/\alpha$ is a strength, not a requirement.
- **Part 4.** $P(\text{left})=0.025$, not $0.1$: exploration picks uniformly among four moves, one of which is left. Learners who give $\varepsilon/3$ have excluded the greedy move from exploration. That is a different rule from the one stated, so the probability is wrong: with the moves right, the criterion is partially met.
