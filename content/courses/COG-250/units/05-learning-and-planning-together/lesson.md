## Outline: Learning and planning together: Dyna

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**.

**Scope.** How an agent can learn a model from experience and plan with it between real steps; what more planning steps buy in the Dyna maze; how an agent's model goes wrong when the world changes, and why optimistic errors are corrected while pessimistic ones may never be; exploration bonuses as a remedy.

**What preparation will add.** A lesson, a laboratory extension that adds learned models and planning steps, original exercises and an exit check. Until then the reading listed for this unit is accurate and the tutor can discuss it, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
