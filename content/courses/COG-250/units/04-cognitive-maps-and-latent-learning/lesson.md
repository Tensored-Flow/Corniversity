## Outline: Cognitive maps and latent learning

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**.

**Scope.** What a cognitive map is claimed to be and what experiments were taken as evidence for one; latent learning as learning without reward that shows when reward is introduced; how a reinforcement-learning environment model restates the idea; and how to read a 1948 paper whose closing section applies the idea to human social life in the language of its time.
