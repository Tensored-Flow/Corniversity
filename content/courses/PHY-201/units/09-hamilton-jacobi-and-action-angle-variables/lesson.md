## Outline: Hamilton–Jacobi theory and action–angle variables

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from the Hamilton–Jacobi and action–angle sections of Chapter 4 of the MIT 8.09 Fall 2014 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; chapter PDF located, page-level locators to be added when the unit is prepared; the 8.09 calendar places this material in lectures 9–13) and the corresponding sections of Chapter 4 of David Tong's *Classical Dynamics* notes (reference-only).

**Scope.** The Hamilton–Jacobi equation as the search for a canonical transformation to constant coordinates, with Hamilton's principal function $S(q,\alpha,t)$ and characteristic function $W(q,\alpha)$; separation of variables, with the harmonic oscillator and the Kepler problem (unit 3 revisited) as worked cases; action variables $J = \oint p\,dq$ and angle variables, the frequencies $\omega = \partial H/\partial J$, and the test for degeneracy; integrable systems and invariant tori as the picture that unit 10 perturbs; adiabatic invariance of $J$ under slow change of parameters (the pendulum of slowly varying length), with the regime of validity stated.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson, original exercises (a Hamilton–Jacobi solution for a separable system, an action-variable computation with the frequency checked against the direct solution, an explain-back on adiabatic invariance), instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
