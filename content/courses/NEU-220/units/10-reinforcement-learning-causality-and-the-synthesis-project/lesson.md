## Outline: reinforcement learning, causality and the synthesis project

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Neuromatch Academy W3D4 Reinforcement Learning and W3D5 Network Causality (content CC BY 4.0, code BSD-3-Clause), citing the exact tutorial pages once they have been opened and verified; so far only the two chapter pages and the course introduction have been. It spans two Neuromatch days and the synthesis week.

**Scope.** Prediction by temporal-difference learning and the reward-prediction-error signal as a normative model (its assumptions and the observations it is compared with kept apart); exploration and exploitation in multi-armed bandits; Q-learning; model-based planning and how it differs from model-free learning (W3D4). Then the question units 3 and 4 kept raising: when does a statistical dependence between neurons license a causal claim? Interventions, the failure of correlation-based connectivity estimates, simultaneous regression and instrumental variables, all on simulated networks whose true connectivity is known (W3D5). The unit closes with guidance for the synthesis project (assessment a4): choose a model from units 5–10, generate synthetic data from a known version of it, fit it, compare with a baseline, show a failure case, and write up exactly what the exercise does and does not show about real neural systems.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics (a derivation of the TD update as stochastic gradient descent on a prediction error, a causality exercise on a stated three-node synthetic network comparing correlation-based and interventional estimates), the project specification with its rubric, instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
