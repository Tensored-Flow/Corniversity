## Reference reasoning (instructor only)

**1. Solution for constant input.** Let $V_\infty = E_L + RI$. Then $u = V - V_\infty$ satisfies $\tau_m\,du/dt = -u$, so $u(t) = u(0)e^{-t/\tau_m}$ and
$$V(t) = V_\infty + (V_{reset} - V_\infty)e^{-t/\tau_m}.$$
$V$ approaches $V_\infty = E_L + RI$ exponentially with time constant $\tau_m$.

**2. Rheobase.** The neuron reaches threshold iff $V_\infty > V_{th}$ (if $V_\infty = V_{th}$ it approaches threshold only asymptotically and never fires). Hence $I > I_{rh} = (V_{th} - E_L)/R$.

**3. Firing rate.** Set $V(T) = V_{th}$: $V_{th} - V_\infty = (V_{reset} - V_\infty)e^{-T/\tau_m}$, so
$$T = \tau_m\ln\frac{V_\infty - V_{reset}}{V_\infty - V_{th}}, \qquad f = \frac{1}{t_{ref} + T}.$$
As $I \to \infty$ the ratio inside the logarithm tends to $1$, $T \to 0$ and $f \to 1/t_{ref}$: the refractory period caps the rate. Just above rheobase $T \to \infty$ and $f \to 0$ continuously (no minimum nonzero rate in the deterministic model).

**4. Numbers.** $I_{rh} = (-50 - (-70))\ \text{mV}/10\ \text{M}\Omega = 20\ \text{mV}/10\ \text{M}\Omega = 2.0$ nA. With $I = 3.0$ nA, $RI = 30$ mV and $V_\infty = -40$ mV. $T = 10\ln\dfrac{-40 - (-65)}{-40 - (-50)} = 10\ln\dfrac{25}{10} = 10\times0.9163 = 9.163$ ms. $f = 1/(2 + 9.163)\ \text{ms} = 1/11.163\ \text{ms} = 89.6$ Hz. (The maximum rate for these parameters is $1/t_{ref} = 500$ Hz.)

**5. Omissions.** Any two of: spike-frequency adaptation under sustained input (add a slow adaptation current or threshold dynamics); the shape of the action potential and the after-hyperpolarisation (add Hodgkin–Huxley sodium and potassium conductances); irregular, CV $\approx 1$ firing under constant mean drive (add noise or stochastic synaptic input, as Tutorial 2 does); subthreshold resonance or dendritic integration (add further compartments or currents). Each addition is a new component with its own testable assumption.

**Alternative valid approaches.** Solving with an integrating factor; nondimensionalising with $v = (V - E_L)/(V_{th} - E_L)$. A learner may give $f$ in the form $1/(t_{ref} + \tau_m\ln(RI/(RI - (V_{th} - E_L))))$ when $V_{reset} = E_L$; here $V_{reset} \ne E_L$, so check that they used the stated reset.

**Common gaps.** Treating the rate as linear in $I$; forgetting the refractory period in $f$; using $V_{reset} = E_L$ when the prompt sets $-65$ mV (gives $T = 10\ln 3 = 11.0$ ms and $f = 77$ Hz, a traceable slip); claiming the model cannot fire for $I$ slightly above rheobase.
