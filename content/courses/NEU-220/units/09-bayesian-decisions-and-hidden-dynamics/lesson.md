## Outline: Bayesian decisions and hidden dynamics

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Neuromatch Academy W3D2 Bayesian Decisions and W3D3 Hidden Dynamics (content CC BY 4.0, code BSD-3-Clause), citing the exact tutorial pages once they have been opened and verified; so far only the two chapter pages have been. It spans two Neuromatch days and is planned as two teaching weeks.

**Scope.** Priors, likelihoods and posteriors for a binary hidden state; loss functions, expected loss and the optimal decision; the continuous case with Gaussian prior and likelihood and the precision-weighted posterior mean; fitting a Bayesian observer model to behavioural data in the bonus tutorial, with the identifiability questions that raises (W3D2). Then states that change: the sequential probability ratio test and evidence accumulation, hidden Markov models with forward inference, and the Kalman filter as recursive Bayesian estimation for a linear-Gaussian dynamical system, which connects back to the linear systems of unit 6 (W3D3). Throughout: a Bayesian observer model is a normative hypothesis about a computation, and the unit will keep its assumptions and its tests separate from claims about neural implementation.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics (a derivation of the Gaussian posterior and the optimal estimate under squared and absolute loss, a derivation of the Kalman gain for a one-dimensional system with a worked synthetic example labelled as such, and a critique of fitting observer models to behaviour), instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
