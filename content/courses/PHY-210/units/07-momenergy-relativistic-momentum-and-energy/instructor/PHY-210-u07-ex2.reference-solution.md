# A sideways photon in a moving detector frame — reference reasoning

Initially $P=(E/c,0,E/c,0)$. With $\gamma=5/4$, $P\prime=(5E/(4c),-3E/(4c),E/c,0)$. Hence $E\prime=5E/4$, direction cosines are $(-3/5,4/5,0)$, and the norm is $[-25/16+9/16+1]E^2/c^2=0$. Generally $p_x\prime=\gamma(E/c)(\cos\theta-\beta)$ and $E\prime/c=\gamma(E/c)(1-\beta\cos\theta)$, giving the stated quotient. The denominator is positive for $|\beta|<1$. Nullness persists and a nonzero photon momentum cannot be brought to zero by an invertible Lorentz matrix; changing photon energy is not acquiring a rest frame.
