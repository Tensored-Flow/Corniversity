# Reconstruct the particle from detector data — reference reasoning

$m^2c^4=(169-9-16)\varepsilon^2=144\varepsilon^2$, so $m=12\varepsilon/c^2$. The velocity is $\mathbf v=(3,4,0)c/13$, with speed $5c/13$ and $\gamma=13/12$. A boost with vector velocity $\mathbf v$ along $(3,4,0)$ takes the particle to rest. With $p_{\parallel}=5\varepsilon/c$, $E\prime=(13/12)(13\varepsilon-(5c/13)(5\varepsilon/c))=12\varepsilon=mc^2$. Its spatial momentum vanishes there. Energy alone could be rest energy of a heavier stationary body or total energy of a lighter moving one; momentum resolves the ambiguity.
