# Two boosts do not generally commute — reference reasoning

For each K the time-space entries of $\eta K$ are antisymmetric, verifying the tangent equation. Multiplication gives $[K_x,K_y]_{12}=1$,$[K_x,K_y]_{21}=-1$, with all other entries zero. Expanding exponentials and retaining the mixed degree 2 term yields $I+ab[K_x,K_y]+O(|a|^2|b|+|a||b|^2)$ near $(a,b)=(0,0)$. This is a spatial infinitesimal rotation in the xy plane (the sign follows our passive-boost convention), fixing time and z to this order. The nonzero commutator proves the two boosts need not commute. Along the same axis the generators commute, allowing rapidity addition; a change of direction needs the full matrix action. No claim is made that a finite commutator is exactly a pure rotation.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
