# Design an approximately inertial experiment — reference reasoning

Transverse relative acceleration is $\ddot L=-(g/R)L=-2.4\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. Thus $\Delta L\simeq-\tfrac12(g/R)Lt^2=-108\,\mu\mathrm m$, exceeding resolution. The duration bound is $t<\sqrt{2\epsilon R/(gL)}=\sqrt{5/12}\,\mathrm s\simeq0.6455\,\mathrm s$. Radial acceleration is $+2(g/R)L$, giving $+216\,\mu\mathrm m$ at3s and the stricter bound $t<\sqrt{\epsilon R/(gL)}\simeq0.4564\,\mathrm s$. Both masses follow free-fall trajectories; accelerometers detect nongravitational proper acceleration, while tides compare neighbouring paths. Require $|\Delta L|\ll L$, small displacement relative to R and negligible other forces.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
