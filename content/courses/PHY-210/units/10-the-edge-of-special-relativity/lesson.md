## Outline: The edge of special relativity — tides, free fall and the road to curvature

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Chapter 9 (Gravity: Curved Spacetime in Action) of Taylor and Wheeler's *Spacetime Physics* (2nd ed., CC BY 4.0; chapter PDF located, page-level locators to be added when the unit is prepared), Section 2.3 of Chapter 2 (Local Character of Free-Float Frame), and the MIT 8.20 Week 4 videos (Lectures 12.1 Equivalence Principle, 12.2 Time Dilation Effect on Earth, 12.3 Bending of Light, 12.4 Redshift Tests, 12.5 General Relativity, 12.6 Experimental Evidence).

**Scope — and a boundary.** This is the one unit of PHY-210 that looks past flat spacetime, and it does so only to say precisely where special relativity stops. Free fall as the free-float frame and the equivalence of inertial and gravitational mass (9.1–9.3); why a free-float frame is only local — the tidal relative acceleration of two separated test particles cannot be transformed away (9.4, 2.3), stated with the Newtonian tidal estimate $\Delta a \approx (2GM/r^3)\Delta r$ and SI numbers; the parable of the two travellers (9.5) as the picture of geodesic deviation; gravitation as curvature of spacetime (9.6) stated as the direction of travel, together with gravitational time dilation and light bending as the observable consequences the equivalence principle already predicts qualitatively; the book's sketches of gravity waves, black holes and cosmology (9.7–9.9) read as orientation only. Nothing about curvature is derived here. Every statement about curved spacetime is labelled as a preview of PHY-240, which begins again from Minkowski space with the tools to do it properly. The unit also closes the course by listing what special relativity has established and under what hypotheses.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson, original exercises (a tidal-acceleration estimate fixing the size of a free-float frame for a given clock precision, a qualitative equivalence-principle argument for gravitational redshift with its hypotheses stated, the closing explain-back on the boundary of the theory), instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
