# Three legs and a maximum-age proof — reference reasoning

The three segments give 3, $\sqrt{25-16}=3$, and 3 years, for 9 years total. The straight route gives 13 years. Since endpoints share $x=0$, any allowed route has $\tau=\int_0^{13}\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}\,dt\leq13$. Equality requires $v=0$ almost everywhere; for a piecewise smooth path any genuine excursion makes the inequality strict. Lorentz proper time uses the difference of time and spatial squares, so the straight route maximises rather than minimises its elapsed time. The hypothesis includes timelike motion and a fixed pair of events in flat spacetime.

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
