# A twin journey seen through received signals — reference reasoning

$\gamma=5/3$ and the turning point is $(5,4)$, so each traveller leg lasts 3 years and total age is 6 years. Simultaneity assignments are $t_E=5-(4/5)4=1.8$ and $t_E=5+(4/5)4=8.2$ years, a 6.4 year change of assigned distant event. During the legs the corresponding Earth increments are 1.8 years each, so 1.8+6.4+1.8=10. Light from turnaround arrives at Earth at 5+4=9 years. The receding Doppler factor is $\sqrt{(1-4/5)/(1+4/5)}=1/3$; approaching it is 3. Earth receives the 3 years of outbound emissions spread over 9 years and 3 years of inbound emissions over 1 year. The observed rate change reaches Earth at year 9, whereas the coordinate simultaneity change is a change of frame convention at the turnaround event.
