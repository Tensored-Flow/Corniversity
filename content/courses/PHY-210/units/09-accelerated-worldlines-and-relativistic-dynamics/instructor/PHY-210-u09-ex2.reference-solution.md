# A four-leg clock comparison — reference reasoning

Each leg contributes $(c/a)\sinh r$ laboratory time. The first two displacements are each $(c^2/a)(\cosh r-1)$; the last two are their negatives. Thus $t_E=4(c/a)\sinh r=3c/a$, $\tau_T=4(c/a)\log2$, $x_{\max}=2(c^2/a)(\cosh r-1)=c^2/(2a)$ and $v_{\max}=c\tanh r=3c/5$. The difference is $(c/a)(3-4\log2)>0$. Minkowski proper-time integrals on two timelike paths suffice; no curvature occurs. The worldline is $C^1$ and piecewise $C^2$, with acceleration jumps at the first and third switches. At the middle switch the acceleration stays $-a$. Finite smooth transitions are physically less idealised and do not restore equal clock readings.
