## Outline: Collisions, decays and invariant mass

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from Chapter 8 (Collide. Create. Annihilate.) of Taylor and Wheeler's *Spacetime Physics* (2nd ed., CC BY 4.0; chapter PDF located, page-level locators to be added when the unit is prepared), with Section 7.5 (Particle Physics: 7.5.1 Particle Decay, 7.5.2 Particle Collisions) of Tong's *Dynamics and Relativity* (reference-only) and the MIT 8.20 videos (Week 3 Lecture 9.3 Collisions; Week 4 Lectures 10.3 Deuteron Production, 10.5 Decay of a Pion, 10.6 Creation of Particles, 10.7 Compton Effect) as supplements.

**Scope.** The system and its total four-momentum (8.1–8.2); the invariant mass of a system, $M^2c^4 = E_{\rm tot}^2 - p_{\rm tot}^2c^2$, which is not the sum of the constituent masses (8.3), with the book's "Dialog: Use and Abuse of the Concept of Mass"; photons as massless carriers of energy and momentum (8.4); creation of mass from photons and from fast particles, and the threshold energies that follow from invariant-mass bookkeeping (8.5–8.6); two-body decay at rest, the energies of the products from invariants, and the pion decay as the worked case; the Compton effect as a four-momentum conservation calculation; fission, fusion and annihilation as conversions between rest energy and kinetic energy (8.7), with SI numbers. The method throughout is: square the conserved four-momentum in the frame where it is simplest, then read off the invariant.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson, original exercises (a threshold-energy derivation checked in the centre-of-momentum frame, a two-body decay with product energies, an explain-back on why mass is not additive), instructor reference reasoning, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
