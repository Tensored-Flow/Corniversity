# Pair creation with a recoiling target — reference reasoning

Initially $s=M^2c^4+2Mc^2E_\gamma$. The least final centre-of-momentum energy is $(M+2m)c^2$, so $E_{\gamma,\rm th}=[(M+2m)^2-M^2]c^2/(2M)=2mc^2(1+m/M)$. At $M=m$, it is $4mc^2$. For $M\gg m$, it is $2mc^2+2m^2c^2/M$, tending to the pair rest energy while retaining a finite recoil correction. A lone photon has null total momentum and $s=0$; a pair of positive-mass particles has $s\geq4m^2c^4>0$, contradicting conservation without a target or additional participant. Rates depend on interaction dynamics and phase space; this calculation gives a kinematic minimum.
