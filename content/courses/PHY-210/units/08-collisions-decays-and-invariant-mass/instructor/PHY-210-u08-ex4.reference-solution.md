# Energy release without a perpetual-motion cycle — reference reasoning

$Q=4.0\times10^{-29}(299792458)^2=3.5950207\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm J$, about 22.44 MeV (22 MeV to the two significant figures of the supplied deficit). Ideal power is $719.004\,\mathrm W\simeq7.2\times10^2\,\mathrm W$ and electrical output about 180 W. Restoring the initial species and states reverses the mass difference, so the reverse Q is negative; at least the released energy must be supplied, with additional losses in practical cycles. Positive release alone does not specify reaction rate, barrier penetration or trigger conditions. For endothermic fixed-target production, momentum conservation can require incident kinetic energy above the rest-mass deficit because the products recoil; use the invariant threshold calculation rather than a scalar rest-energy comparison.

## Assessment note

Accept any correct alternative derivation with equivalent stated conventions. A numerical check supports a calculation; it does not prove a general claim. Diagnose the first substantive gap, and do not equate help-assisted work with independent demonstration.
