# Unequal daughters from a particle at rest — reference reasoning

With $P_2=P-P_1$, squaring gives $E_1=(M^2+m_1^2-m_2^2)c^2/(2M)$ and the analogous $E_2$. For the given masses,$E_1=11mc^2/5$ and $E_2=14mc^2/5$, which sum to $5mc^2$. The common momentum is $p=(mc/5)\sqrt{121-25}=4\sqrt6\,mc/5$. Then $v_1=4\sqrt6\,c/11$ and $v_2=2\sqrt6\,c/7$, both below c. The shells give $(11/5)^2-(4\sqrt6/5)^2=1$ and $(14/5)^2-(4\sqrt6/5)^2=4$. Opposite momentum directions are fixed relative to each other, but the angular distribution and lifetime require interaction and spin information, not just conservation.
