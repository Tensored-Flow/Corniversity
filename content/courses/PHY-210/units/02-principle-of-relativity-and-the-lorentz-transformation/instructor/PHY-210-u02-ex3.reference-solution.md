## Reference explanation and acceptable alternatives (instructor only)

Explanation exercise for a mathematically trained learner; grade the ideas, give enough feedback to resolve the first substantive gap.

- Must contain: boosts preserve the indefinite form $-c^2t^2 + x^2$ (metric $\eta = \mathrm{diag}(-1,1)$); the set of boosts along one axis is $\{\Lambda(\phi):\phi\in\mathbb R\}$, a one-parameter group isomorphic to $(\mathbb R,+)$ with $\Lambda(\phi_1)\Lambda(\phi_2) = \Lambda(\phi_1+\phi_2)$; velocity is $c\tanh\phi$, so velocities combine by the tanh addition formula and never reach $c$.
- Physical content of "rapidities add": composing uniform motions multiplies boost matrices; the additive parameter is the hyperbolic angle, and the bound $|\tanh\phi|<1$ is the statement that no composition of sub-light velocities exceeds $c$.
- Breakdown of the analogy, any one of: the group is non-compact ($SO^+(1,1)$, $\phi$ unbounded, no periodicity, no return to the identity); the preserved form is indefinite, so there are null vectors and three causal types that no boost can interchange; boosts in different directions do not commute and their composition involves a spatial rotation (Thomas–Wigner), unlike planar rotations.
- Do not require the names $SO(1,1)$ or $SO^+(1,3)$; do require the correct quadratic form with the minus sign.

## One possible learner explanation

A boost along one axis preserves the indefinite quadratic form $-(ct)^2+x^2$, rather than Euclidean length. Its matrix entries are hyperbolic sine and cosine of a real rapidity. Matrix multiplication adds rapidities because the hyperbolic addition identities reproduce the product. Thus these boosts form a one-parameter group isomorphic to the additive real numbers. Velocity is $c\tanh\phi$, so adding rapidities induces the nonlinear velocity-addition rule and keeps any finite composition below light speed. Unlike an ordinary plane rotation, increasing rapidity is not periodic: no nonzero rapidity returns the boost to identity. Noncollinear boosts require the larger Lorentz group and need not commute.
