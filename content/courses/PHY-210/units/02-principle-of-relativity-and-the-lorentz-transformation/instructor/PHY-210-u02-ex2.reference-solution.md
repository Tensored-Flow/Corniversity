## Reference reasoning (instructor only)

$v = 0.600c$, $\gamma = 1/\sqrt{1 - 0.36} = 1/0.800 = 1.25$. Barn frame $S$ (barn at rest, pole moving in $+x$); pole frame $S'$.

**1. Lengths.** Barn frame measures the pole: $10.0/1.25 = 8.00\ \mathrm m$. Pole frame measures the barn: $8.00/1.25 = 6.40\ \mathrm m$. Each frame measures the other's object contracted; both statements are about simultaneous end-position measurements in the measuring frame.

**2. Barn-frame events.** Front door at $x = 0$, back door at $x = 8.00\ \mathrm m$. In $S$ the pole is $8.00\ \mathrm m$ long, so at the barn-time when its front is at the back door its rear is at the front door: $E_1 = (ct, x) = (0, 8.00\ \mathrm m)$, $E_2 = (0, 0)$, simultaneous in $S$ by construction (the contracted pole exactly spans the barn).

**3. Pole-frame events.** $ct' = \gamma(ct - (v/c)x)$: $ct'_1 = 1.25\,(0 - 0.600\times8.00) = -6.00\ \mathrm m$, $ct'_2 = 0$. So $E_1$ occurs first in the pole frame, $6.00\ \mathrm m$ of light-travel time $= 6.00/0.299\,792\,458 = 20.0\ \mathrm{ns}$ before $E_2$. Consistency: in $S'$ the pole is $10.0\ \mathrm m$ and the barn $6.40\ \mathrm m$, moving at $0.600c$ in the $-x'$ direction. At $E_1$ the barn's back door is at the pole's front; the barn's front door is then $6.40\ \mathrm m$ from the pole's front, i.e. $3.60\ \mathrm m$ short of the pole's rear. It covers $3.60\ \mathrm m$ at $0.600c$ in $3.60/0.600 = 6.00\ \mathrm m$ of light-travel time. $\checkmark$ (Positions too: $x'_1 = \gamma(x_1 - vt_1) = 1.25\times8.00 = 10.0\ \mathrm m$ and $x'_2 = 0$ — the pole's two ends, as they must be.)

**4. Interval.** $\Delta s^2 = -0 + (8.00)^2 = +64.0\ \mathrm{m^2} > 0$: spacelike; proper distance $8.00\ \mathrm m$ (the barn's proper length — the events are simultaneous in the barn frame, so that is where the proper distance is measured). The time order of a spacelike pair is frame-dependent; "the pole fits" means "$E_1$ and $E_2$ are simultaneous or $E_2$ precedes $E_1$", which is true in the barn frame and false in the pole frame, with no contradiction. Frames moving in the $-x$ direction relative to the barn (e.g. a second pole thrown the other way) have $ct' = \gamma(ct + (|v|/c)x)$ and see $E_2$ before $E_1$. Nothing physical depends on the order, because no signal connects two spacelike-separated events.

**Alternative valid approaches.** Choosing the time origin differently or putting the back door at $x = 0$ changes coordinates, not differences; accept any consistent choice. A spacetime diagram with the two worldlines of the doors and the two of the pole's ends is an excellent supplement (unit 4 formalises it).

**Common gaps.** Contracting the wrong object in one of the frames; a sign error giving $E_2$ first in the pole frame (catch it with the consistency check: the barn must still move toward the pole's rear after $E_1$); forgetting that the proper distance between the events is the barn's proper length, not the pole's; concluding "therefore the pole really does not fit" (flagged by the automatic check).
