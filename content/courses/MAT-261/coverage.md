# Coverage and assessment map

All twelve units are authored. Exact readings and explicit proof portfolios form part of the course. Each exercise is linked to an activity and has an instructor reference. Estimates are provisional; accepted work evidences only the arguments actually assessed. New post-review additions require independent rechecking.

## MAT-261-u01: Topological bridge and the definition of a smooth manifold

- State the definition of a smooth n-manifold with all three ingredients (topological axioms, charts, smooth overlaps) and explain the role of each.
- Verify Hausdorffness, second countability and compactness for subspaces of Euclidean space and for quotients, using open maps and saturated open sets.
- Compute transition maps for the stereographic charts on S^n and the affine charts on RP^n and prove smooth compatibility.
- Prove that every atlas lies in a unique maximal atlas without Zorn's lemma, and explain when two atlases define the same manifold.

Exit/synthesis: `MAT-261-u01-ex1`. Unit scheduled estimate: 290 minutes, including reading and workshops.

- `MAT-261-u01-ex1` — Stereographic charts on the sphere; 45 minutes; existing independent/explain-back task.
- `MAT-261-u01-ex2` — Real projective space is a smooth manifold; 50 minutes; existing independent/explain-back task.
- `MAT-261-u01-ex3` — Maximal atlases without Zorn's lemma; 30 minutes; existing independent/explain-back task.
## MAT-261-u02: Smooth maps, the standard pathologies and the derivative

- Define smooth maps and diffeomorphisms between manifolds and prove that smoothness can be tested with one atlas on each side.
- Construct the line with two origins, show it has a smooth atlas and is second countable but not Hausdorff, and derive the failure of unique limits.
- Show that the charts x and x^3 on R give different smooth structures that are nonetheless diffeomorphic, and explain the distinction between structures and diffeomorphism classes.
- State the definition of the differential, prove its uniqueness, and define rank, immersion and submersion with chart-independence justified.

Exit/synthesis: `MAT-261-u02-ex4`. Unit scheduled estimate: 320 minutes, including reading and workshops.

- `MAT-261-u02-ex4` — A smooth circle map and coordinate-independent rank; 70 minutes; synthesis.
