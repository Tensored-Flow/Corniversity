## Outline: Sard's theorem and stratified spaces

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from lectures 10–12 of the MIT 18.965 Fall 2004 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keeping the notes' statement numbering (Theorems 12.1 and 12.3, Definition 13.1), with Hitchin's appendix §10.5 (reference-only) for the equidimensional special case.

**Scope.** Measure zero for subsets of manifolds and its invariance under smooth maps between manifolds of the same dimension; the measure-zero criterion of Theorem 12.1; Sard's theorem (Theorem 12.3) for a smooth map of finite-dimensional manifolds, with the inductive proof; density of regular values and first consequences for embeddings and transversality; stratified spaces (Definition 13.1) and their examples.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
