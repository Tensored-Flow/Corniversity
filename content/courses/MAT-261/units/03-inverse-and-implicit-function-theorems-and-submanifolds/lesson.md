## Outline: inverse and implicit function theorems; submanifolds

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from lectures 4–5 of the MIT 18.965 Fall 2004 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keeping the notes' statement numbering (Theorem 5.1, Theorem 5.2, Definition 5.3, Corollary 5.4) so that every claim can be cited to an exact page, with Hitchin's appendix §10.1 as a second proof of the inverse function theorem.

**Scope.** The inverse function theorem proved by the contraction mapping principle; the implicit function theorem in Banach form and the local normal forms of immersions and submersions; critical points, regular values and the theorem that the preimage of a regular value is a submanifold; the orthogonal group $O(n)$ as a manifold of dimension $n(n-1)/2$; existence and uniqueness for ordinary differential equations obtained from the inverse function theorem.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
