## Grading notes (instructor only)

- Automatic checks: `transition-inversion` looks for $y/|y|^2$ (or $|y|^{-2}$, or the word "inversion"); `no-single-chart` flags the claim that one chart covers $S^n$. Advisory only; read the argument.
- The first substantive gap is usually in part 3: the overlap image must be $\mathbb{R}^n\setminus\{0\}$, and the learner must say why the transition map is smooth *there* (denominator $|y|^2\neq0$). Quote the line.
- Part 2: a learner who derives the inverse but forgets to show $\varphi_+$ is onto $\mathbb{R}^n$ has shown injectivity only; mark "partially met" on `inverse` with a precise comment.
- Part 4: "compact is not homeomorphic to non-compact" is not enough as stated, because $\varphi(S^n)$ is a priori only an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$; the argument needs open + closed + connectedness (or that a compact open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$ is empty). High confidence when this is explicit; medium when only "compactness" is mentioned.
- Confidence: high when the inverse formula, the overlap image and the closed form all appear; route to `needs_review` if the learner uses invariance of domain or other heavy theorems whose statements are not quoted correctly.
- Do not penalise the choice of which coordinate carries the poles, or notational variants ($\mathbf{x}$, $\hat{x}$, $x_0$).
