## Lesson notes (instructor only)

Source mapping, 18.965 lecture 1 notes (Mrowka, Fall 2004; PDF pages, no printed labels): standing hypothesis and Definitions 1.1–1.2 (p. 1); atlases, equivalence, maximality, Exercise 1 (p. 2); Definition 1.3 and §1.1 examples $\mathbb{R}^n$, $S^n$, $\mathbb{RP}^n$, $\mathbb{CP}^n$ (p. 3); Exercise 2, Definition 1.4, Exercises 3–4 on Grassmannians (p. 4). Hitchin 2014: §2.1 (p. 6), §2.2 (p. 9), §2.3 (p. 11).

Teaching intent: the unit installs the habit of separating three checks (topology, charts, overlaps). Keep the tutorial on exercise 2 until the learner has produced disjoint *saturated* open sets for two lines; "quotients of Hausdorff spaces are Hausdorff" is the error to catch in the same discussion, with the line with two origins (unit 2) as the counterexample.

Mrowka's Definition 1.1 says "homeomorphism onto its image"; the lesson adds "open subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$" explicitly so that invariance of domain is never needed. If the learner asks, say so and point to Hitchin's Definition 1, which requires openness.

The lesson deliberately does not state the inverse of the stereographic chart, the transition map $y/|y|^2$, or the Hausdorff argument for $\mathbb{RP}^n$. If the learner has seen these before (likely), move quickly to the alternative-approach questions in the reference notes and to exercise 3.

Staged misconception for a peer discussion, if peers are enabled: a classmate may claim that $S^n$ "is covered by one chart if we send the pole to infinity"; resolve by asking what the chart's image would be and using compactness.
