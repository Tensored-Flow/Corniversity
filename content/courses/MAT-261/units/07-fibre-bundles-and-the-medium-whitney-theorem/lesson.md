## Outline: fibre bundles and the medium Whitney embedding theorem

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from lectures 13–14 of the MIT 18.965 Fall 2004 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keeping the notes' statement numbering (Definition 14.2, Theorem 15.1).

**Scope.** Locally trivial fibre bundles $(E,B,p)$ with fibre $F$, their trivialisations and structure groups, with examples from projective spaces, Grassmannians and the tangent bundle. Whitney's embedding theorem in its medium version: a compact $n$-manifold embeds in $\mathbb{R}^{2n+1}$, obtained from the easiest version (unit 5) by projecting along generic directions, where Sard's theorem (unit 6) shows that the bad directions, those in the image of the secant map or of the tangent map, form a set of measure zero.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
