## Outline: Banach-space interlude, Fredholm operators and Smale's Sard theorem

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from lectures 15–19 of the MIT 18.965 Fall 2004 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keeping the notes' statement numbering (§16.1–16.3, §17).

**Scope.** The linear analysis the course uses when the model space is infinite-dimensional: normed and Banach spaces, bounded operators and completions, the Hahn–Banach, open mapping, Banach isomorphism and closed graph theorems stated as tools; compact operators and the Arzelà–Ascoli theorem; Fredholm operators, the index and its stability; Banach manifolds, residual sets and Smale's Sard theorem for Fredholm maps between second countable Banach manifolds. A learner who has completed Functional Analysis I (MAT-230) will recognise most of §16.1–16.2 and should concentrate on the Fredholm theory and on §17.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
