## Outline: the Lie derivative, the Frobenius theorem and foliations

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from lectures 29–30 of the MIT 18.965 Fall 2004 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keeping the notes' statement numbering (§21.1 and Theorem 21.3, Theorem 21.4, Definition 21.5, §§22–24), with Hitchin §4.3–4.4 (reference-only) for flows and the Lie bracket.

**Scope.** Flows of vector fields and the Lie derivative of functions, vector fields and tensor fields; the canonical form of a non-vanishing vector field; distributions, involutivity and the Frobenius integrability theorem, with the local canonical form of an integrable distribution; foliations and their leaves; codimension-one foliations characterised by a normal vector field; the holonomy of a closed loop in a leaf; Reeb's stability theorem for compact leaves with finite fundamental group.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
