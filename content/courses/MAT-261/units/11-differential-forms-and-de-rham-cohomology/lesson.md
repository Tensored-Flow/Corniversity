## Outline: differential forms, the Poincaré lemma and de Rham cohomology

This unit is **outlined, not yet prepared**. It will be written from lectures 31–32 of the MIT 18.965 Fall 2004 notes (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keeping the notes' statement numbering (§25, Theorem 25.1, Definition 25.3, §25.2, §26), with Hitchin §5–6 (reference-only) as the fuller treatment of the exterior algebra, forms and the exterior derivative.

**Scope.** The exterior algebra $\Lambda^\bullet V^*$ and its universal property; differential $p$-forms as sections of $\Lambda^pT^*M$; the wedge product and pullback; the exterior derivative defined in coordinates and characterised invariantly, with the proof that $d^2=0$; cochain complexes and the de Rham cohomology groups $H^p_{dR}(M)$; the Poincaré lemma for star-shaped open sets via a homotopy operator; homotopy invariance of de Rham cohomology; the definition of the Čech cohomology of an open cover, continued in unit 12.

**What preparation will add.** A full lesson with definitions, examples and non-examples, the main theorems with their hypotheses in view, original exercises with hint ladders and rubrics, instructor reference reasoning for the assessor, an exit check and the unit's common misconceptions. Until then the reading list above is accurate and the tutor can discuss the material, but there is no assessed work for this unit.
